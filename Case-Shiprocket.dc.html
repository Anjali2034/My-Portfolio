01 · Problem

A charge the seller pays but can't check.

The courier sets the failure reason, Shiprocket bills on it, and the courier judges the dispute. A false reason goes unnoticed.
How I got here

I found this while preparing for an interview. I don't run a store, so seller reviews became my interviews, and I checked each claim in a live seller account.

03 · Root cause

Four reasons a false attempt goes unnoticed.

01
Self reported
The party at fault picks the reason. OTP proof exists but isn't required.
02
Wired to revenue
The buyer's “no one came” books a paid reattempt, not a courier check.
03
Courier is the judge
Weight disputes are “accepted or rejected by courier”.
04
No reasons in the wallet
Sellers see debits, not proof, so every doubt becomes a ticket.
04 · Solution

Let missing proof move the money.

Every failed attempt gets a proof status. No proof plus a buyer dispute counts as courier fault.
