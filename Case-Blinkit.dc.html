01 · Problem

Orders grow. Baskets don't.

Blinkit stocks electronics, but never says what happens if they break. One failed return teaches a customer to keep it for groceries.
How I got here

I bought an iron for my sister on Blinkit. It had an electrical fault. I sent photos, was asked for live photos, called support many times, and it was never replaced or refunded.

STEP 1
Read 600 reviews
About 51 of 411 low-star ones are electronics
STEP 2
Audited the site
Home, search and an earbuds page
STEP 3
Tested discovery
“Gift for dad under 1000” worked well
STEP 4
The real gap
People can find products. They can't trust them.

03 · Root cause

Four reasons trust breaks.

01
Promise is hidden
The policy sits in product details, written for legal cover.
02
Promise is inconsistent
Some brands replace fast, others send you to a service centre.
03
Proof is on the buyer
Photos, then live photos, then videos, with no record at handover.
04
No social proof
No ratings, and 81 to 86% off MRP looks suspicious.
04 · Solution

Answer the question before they pay.

A promise Blinkit itself backs, shown on the card, page and cart.
